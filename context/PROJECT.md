# PROJECT.md

## Original Problem Statement (Aaron, HW1)

Students are struggling to get a job in the tech industry due to a gap in skills used by employers.

Team scope: this also covers internships and co-ops for students who are not graduating into full-time roles. It covers technical and business roles in the tech industry, not only software and data engineering.

## Reframed for the Team

A student targeting a role in the tech industry reads postings that list tools and skills. A software or data posting names tools like Databricks, Snowflake, and Docker. A product, analytics, or operations posting names SQL, Tableau, Jira, or Salesforce. The lists differ by company and change every hiring cycle. The student cannot tell which skill to learn next, so they guess, and a wrong guess costs a semester they cannot get back.

**What the reframe changed.** The original statement treats an offer as the end of the problem. The problem now belongs to the student at the moment of choosing what to learn next. Solved now means a student can name their next skill, tie it to the roles they are targeting, and say when they have learned enough for now. We can observe that in three weeks. An offer depends on the market, so it stays as the long-run outcome we will not move during the course.

**Why it is still wicked.** There is no stopping rule, because employers change the skills they ask for faster than any student can finish learning them. A choice of skills is good or bad, not true or false, and the answer changes with the role. The student is liable for the result. A missed requirement costs them the offer, not the school. Advice that was right last year can be wrong this year.

## Outcome

Increase the share of students who leave a session able to name their next skill, the role it serves, and why. No baseline has been measured yet.

## Scope

- In: a student states their major, what they already know, and the roles they target, and sees a short ranked list of what to learn next.
- Out: course enrollment, job applications, paid job-posting data, accounts for students.

## Constraints

- Course stack: Cloudflare Workers, D1, static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Three weeks of build time in Phase 2.
- Data the team can get legally and soon.
